The game uses Ogg Vorbis encodes. The Moonlit Arena track was converted from the
original WAV. The Red Rock Canyon and menu tracks were trimmed at the end,
level-matched, and given a short crossfade at the loop boundary. The Bamboo
Walkways track uses the published seamless loop file as provided.
